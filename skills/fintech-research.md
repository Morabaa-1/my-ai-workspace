# Skill: FinTech Research and Analysis

## Objective
Conduct structured, evidence-based research into financial technology markets, products, regulations and emerging technologies.

## Research Workflow

### Step 1: Define the Research Question
Identify the research problem, geographical scope and intended outcome.

### Step 2: Identify Sources
Prioritise:
- Central banks and financial regulators
- Government legislation and policy documents
- Academic journals and conference papers
- World Bank and IMF publications
- Reputable industry reports

### Step 3: Analyse Evidence
Evaluate:
- Market conditions
- Regulatory requirements
- Technology infrastructure
- Customer behaviour
- Business opportunities
- Risks and limitations

### Step 4: Compare and Critically Evaluate
Identify similarities, differences, gaps and conflicting evidence.

### Step 5: Develop Recommendations
Provide practical recommendations supported by evidence.

## Output Format
1. Executive summary
2. Research context
3. Evidence and findings
4. Critical analysis
5. Opportunities and risks
6. Recommendations
7. References

## Quality Controls
- Never invent sources or citations.
- Distinguish primary and secondary sources.
- Verify legislation and publication dates.
- Highlight research limitations.
- Use Harvard referencing when requested.
