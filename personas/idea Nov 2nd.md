# Idea — 2 November: Green Trades Talent Pipeline

## The concept

Pivot NextSkill from consumer-paid trade taster sessions to a B2B **Green Trades Talent Pipeline**.

Employers, retrofit contractors, colleges, local authorities and workforce organisations pay for practical programmes that help people discover and move into entry-level green-construction careers.

## The offer

A half-day or one-day "try, assess and route" experience for a cohort, including:

- Hands-on introductions to electrical, retrofit, heating or construction work.
- A realistic job preview from working tradespeople.
- A simple interest and aptitude assessment.
- Clear routes into apprenticeships, pre-apprenticeships, jobs and further training.
- A partner report covering attendance, candidate profiles, interest, readiness and follow-up actions.

## Why it is promising

- It addresses a concrete workforce-shortage problem in Irish construction and retrofit.
- The customer is an organisation with a recruitment, training or workforce-development budget, rather than an individual deciding whether to spend €39–€45.
- It preserves the strongest part of NextSkill: hands-on career discovery.
- It can generate higher-value partner contracts (indicatively €2,000–€7,500) and be validated through a paid pilot before investing in a platform or permanent workshop.

## Recommended first pilot

Launch a **Retrofit Careers Discovery Day** for 18–30-year-olds or career changers, in partnership with one training provider and two or three local contractors.

The partner funds the cohort; attendance is free or low-cost for participants. Measure attendance, completion, apprenticeship or interview applications, and partner satisfaction.

## Positioning

> We help employers turn local curiosity into a qualified, motivated pipeline for green-trade careers.

## Evidence to revisit

- Ireland's construction and retrofit plans require a substantially expanded workforce and stronger apprenticeship/training capacity.
- The national retrofit programme targets 500,000 home upgrades and 400,000 heat pumps by 2030.
- Construction employers identify attracting and retaining talent, including a more diverse workforce, as a major constraint.
