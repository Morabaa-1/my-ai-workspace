# Kenya FinTech Regulation and Digital Lending

## Research Context
Kenya has an established digital financial services ecosystem, including mobile money, digital credit providers and technology-enabled financial services.

## Key Regulatory Frameworks

### 1. Data Protection Act, 2019
Establishes requirements for processing personal data, including lawful processing, transparency, data subject rights and safeguards concerning automated decision-making.

### 2. Central Bank of Kenya (Digital Credit Providers) Regulations, 2022
Establishes regulatory requirements for digital credit providers operating within the scope of the regulations.

### 3. Consumer Protection
Digital lenders must consider fair treatment, transparency, disclosure of lending costs and responsible lending practices.

### 4. Competition and Consumer Protection
Competition and consumer protection requirements are relevant to pricing transparency, market conduct and customer treatment.

## Key Research Questions
- How do digital lenders collect and process customer data?
- What safeguards apply to automated credit decisions?
- How can borrowers challenge inaccurate credit assessments?
- How can regulators balance innovation and consumer protection?
- What are the implications of alternative data in credit scoring?

## Potential FinTech Opportunities
- Responsible digital lending infrastructure
- SME reconciliation and settlement solutions
- Financial data governance tools
- Alternative credit assessment with appropriate safeguards
- Regulatory compliance automation

Any product or business proposal should be checked against current legislation, applicable regulatory guidance and licensing requirements.
